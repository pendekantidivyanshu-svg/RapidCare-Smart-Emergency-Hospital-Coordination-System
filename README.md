# 🚑 RapidCare

RapidCare is a smart emergency hospital coordination system designed to reduce treatment delays during medical emergencies.

Instead of taking patients to the nearest hospital without knowing resource availability, RapidCare allows ambulance staff to send patient vitals and emergency requirements to nearby hospitals. Hospitals automatically check their available resources and respond instantly, enabling ambulances to transport patients directly to the most suitable hospital.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During emergencies, ambulances often transport patients to the nearest hospital without knowing whether the hospital has:

- ICU beds
- Trauma specialists
- Ventilators
- Blood availability
- Operation theatres
- Emergency treatment capacity

If the hospital lacks the required resources, valuable time is lost transferring the patient to another hospital.

RapidCare aims to solve this problem by enabling real-time communication between ambulances and hospitals before patient arrival.
